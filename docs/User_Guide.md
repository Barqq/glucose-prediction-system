# User Guide: AI-Powered Post-Meal Glucose Prediction System

## Welcome to the AI-Powered Post-Meal Glucose Prediction System!

This guide will help you navigate and utilize all the features of our innovative application, designed to empower you in managing your diabetes with precision and ease. Our system provides accurate post-meal blood glucose predictions and personalized insulin dose recommendations, all within an intuitive, Arabic-first, iOS-style interface.

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Account Creation and Login

1.  **Download the App:** (Once available on the App Store).
2.  **Sign Up:** Upon first launch, you will be guided through a simple sign-up process. You will need to provide your email, create a password, and fill in some basic health information (age, weight, height, type of diabetes, carbohydrate ratio, correction factor, and target glucose level). This information is crucial for personalizing your predictions.
3.  **Login:** After creating your account, you can log in using your registered email and password.

### 1.2 Onboarding

Our interactive onboarding flow will introduce you to the app's key features and guide you through initial setup steps, including granting necessary permissions (e.g., camera access for meal analysis).

## 2. Dashboard (Home Screen)

The Dashboard is your central hub for managing your diabetes. Here you will find an overview of your glucose trends and quick access to key features.

*   **Greeting:** A personalized greeting message and the current date.
*   **Glucose Chart:** A visual representation of your simulated continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) data, showing your glucose levels over time. You can typically view trends for the last 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days.
*   **Quick Action Buttons:**
    *   **"تصوير الوجبة" (Photograph Meal):** Tap this to quickly access the Meal Photo Analyzer.
    *   **"حساب الأنسولين" (Calculate Insulin):** Tap this to go directly to the Insulin Calculator.
*   **Recent Activity:** A chronological list of your recent meals, insulin doses, and glucose readings.
*   **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Provides easy access to the main sections of the app: Dashboard, Meal Analyzer, Insulin Calculator, History, and Profile.

## 3. Meal Photo Analyzer

This feature uses advanced AI to identify your meals and estimate their nutritional content, which is vital for accurate glucose prediction.

1.  **Accessing the Analyzer:** Tap "تصوير الوجبة" (Photograph Meal) from the Dashboard or the navigation bar.
2.  **Capture Your Meal:** Position your camera over your meal. Ensure good lighting and try to capture the entire meal clearly. The app may offer guidelines for optimal photo taking (e.g., top-down view).
3.  **Analyze:** After capturing the photo, the system will process it to identify food items and estimate portion sizes and macronutrients (carbohydrates, proteins, fats).
4.  **Review Results:** The app will display the identified food items, their estimated nutritional content, and a predicted post-meal glucose curve based on this meal. You can review and confirm these details.
5.  **Confirm and Log:** Confirm the meal details to log it into your history and use it for further predictions.

## 4. Insulin Calculator

Receive precise, AI-powered recommendations for your bolus insulin dose.

1.  **Accessing the Calculator:** Tap "حساب الأنسولين" (Calculate Insulin) from the Dashboard or the navigation bar.
2.  **Input Data:** You will be prompted to enter your current blood glucose level. The estimated carbohydrates from your last analyzed meal will be pre-filled, but you can adjust them if needed. Your target glucose level, carbohydrate ratio, and correction factor (from your profile) will be used in the calculation.
3.  **Calculate:** The system will use its AI models to calculate your recommended bolus insulin dose.
4.  **Review Recommendation:** The app will display the recommended dose, often broken down into carbohydrate coverage and correction dose. It may also show a refined predicted glucose curve.
5.  **Confirm and Log:** Confirm the recommended dose to log it into your history.

## 5. Early Warning Engine

The system continuously monitors your data and provides proactive alerts for potential hypoglycemic (low blood sugar) or hyperglycemic (high blood sugar) events.

*   **Alert Banners:** These will appear prominently on your screen (e.g., Dashboard) with color-coded severity indicators.
*   **Recommendations:** Each alert will come with actionable recommendations (e.g., "Check blood sugar," "Consider a small snack," "Consult your doctor").

## 6. History

Review your past activities, meals, glucose readings, and insulin doses.

1.  **Accessing History:** Tap "السجل" (History) from the navigation bar.
2.  **Filter Options:** Use the filter options to view data by date range, meal type, or other criteria.
3.  **Timeline View:** Browse through a chronological list of your logged entries. Each entry will provide a summary of the event.
4.  **Detailed View:** Tap on any entry to view more detailed information, such as meal analysis results, glucose curves, or insulin dose calculations.

## 7. Profile

Manage your personal information, health settings, and app preferences.

1.  **Accessing Profile:** Tap "الملف الشخصي" (Profile) from the navigation bar.
2.  **User Information:** View and update your personal details.
3.  **Health Settings:** Adjust your carbohydrate ratio, correction factor, target glucose level, and other diabetes-related parameters. **Consult your healthcare provider before making significant changes to these settings.**
4.  **App Settings:** Configure language preferences, notification settings, and data synchronization options.
5.  **Support and Legal:** Access links to our help center, privacy policy, and terms of service.

## 8. Security and Privacy

Your data security and privacy are our top priorities. We employ robust encryption, secure authentication, and adhere to strict data protection regulations, including SFDA guidelines. Your health information is kept confidential and used solely to provide you with personalized diabetes management support.

## 9. Troubleshooting

*   **Inaccurate Predictions:** Ensure your health profile settings (carb ratio, correction factor) are up-to-date and accurate. Review your meal photos for clarity and correct food identification. If issues persist, consult your healthcare provider.
*   **App Not Responding:** Try restarting the app. If the problem continues, check your internet connection or contact support.
*   **Camera Issues:** Ensure the app has camera permissions enabled in your device settings.

## 10. Contact Support

If you encounter any issues or have questions not covered in this guide, please visit our help center or contact our support team through the app's Profile section.
